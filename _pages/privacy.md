This website uses Google Analytics to understand aggregate traffic and MapMyVisitors to display an approximate geographic map of visits. These services may receive technical information such as an IP address, approximate location, browser or device type, referring page, and pages viewed. The visitor map does not display precise addresses, and I do not use these services to identify individual visitors.

MapMyVisitors states that its embedded widget does not set cookies or track visitors across different websites. Its processing and retention practices are described in the [MapMyVisitors privacy policy](https://mapmyvisitors.com/b/policy). Google describes its analytics data practices in [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
