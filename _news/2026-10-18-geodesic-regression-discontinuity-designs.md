Our paper [Regression Discontinuity Designs for Functional Data and Random Objects in Geodesic Spaces](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.18136) will appear in the **Journal of the Royal Statistical Society: Series B**. We extend regression discontinuity designs to functional data and other random objects in geodesic spaces, with theory and applications to air-quality curves and voting compositions.
