[*Quantifying Centrality for Complex Data*](https://neurips.cc/virtual/2026/poster/150293) has been accepted to **NeurIPS 2026**. The paper introduces centrality scores based on distance profiles and optimal transport for identifying central and atypical observations in complex-data settings.
