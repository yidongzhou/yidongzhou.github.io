I am pleased to share that our paper, [Sliced Wasserstein Regression](https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.10601), has been accepted for publication in the **Journal of Machine Learning Research**. The paper develops global and local regression methods for multivariate distributional responses using the sliced Wasserstein distance, with theoretical guarantees and applications to excess winter mortality and financial data.
