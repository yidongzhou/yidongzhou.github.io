Our paper [Geodesic Difference-in-Differences](https://doi.org/10.1093/biomet/asag054) will appear in **Biometrika**. It extends difference-in-differences methods to structured outcomes, including distributions, networks, and manifold-valued data, with applications to age-at-death distributions and electricity-generation compositions.
